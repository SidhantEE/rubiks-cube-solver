Drive adapter · 16.5 mm tip · 17HE19-2004S shaft

The shaft hole is too tight

On the trial adapter, only the rounded-off end of the shaft goes in, about 1 mm. The full 5 mm shaft should slide in until its round part reaches the flat, with about 6.5 mm of shaft still showing. The animation below shows that.

Next print: drive_adapter_bore_set.stl is six adapters, all with the 16.5 mm tip, with shaft holes drawn at 5.3, 5.4, 5.5, 5.6, 5.7 and 5.8 mm. Each has its hole size engraved on the side. The hole on your trial print was drawn at 5.2 mm and came out smaller, as printed holes do. Use the smallest one the shaft goes all the way into.

Why the round part can't go further

End views, drawn to scale (about 34× life size).

The hole is D-shaped from 2.5 mm in, to match the 15 mm flat on the shaft. The round part of the shaft is 5 mm across, but past the flat the hole is only 4.75 mm across. So the round part goes 2.5 mm in and stops. The tip of the shaft reaches the bottom of the hole at the same point.

Putting it on

  1. Line up the shaft's flat with the small set-screw hole on the side of the adapter. The flat inside the hole faces that side.
  2. Push until it stops. About 6.5 mm of round shaft will still show. If it stops after only 1 or 2 mm, the hole is too small: try the next size up.
  3. In the robot, the motor bolts to the 6 mm plate first, then the adapter goes on from the cube side and stops 1.5 mm clear of the plate.
  4. Tighten an M3 set screw onto the flat. It cuts its own thread in the plastic.
