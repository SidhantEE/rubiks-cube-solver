Cube Solver Robot
Bill of Materials

Six NEMA 17 steppers, one per face, each coupled straight to a center piece. A webcam reads the colors, Python finds a solution in about 20 moves, and an Arduino Mega turns the faces.

One motor per face

Unfold the cube and every face has its own motor and its own pair of Mega pins. A move like R' spins only the right motor 90° counter-clockwise, which is 400 microsteps at 1/8 stepping.

Webcam→scanner.py→two-phase solve→USB serial→Mega 2560→6× TMC2209→6× NEMA 17
